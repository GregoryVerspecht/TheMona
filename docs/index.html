The Story

Every great project starts with an idea. Here's the journey of The Mona.

Inspiration

This project was born out of a love for creativity, technology, and the joy of bringing people together. The initial spark for The Mona came after we encountered "Odil de Krokodil," a concept that quickly became a beloved part of our friend group. Inspired by the positive reception, we created our own version, "Lucien," which was met with even more enthusiasm.

Why "The Mona"?

When searching for a name, I wanted something that reflected a meaningful source of joy and inspiration in my life. The Mona carries the memory of someone who, in ways they may never fully realize, reignited a light in me I thought was lost. They reminded me of the quiet beauty in small moments and the strength in simply feeling alive again.

This project captures the essence of those feelings, transforming a fleeting connection into a creative expression of gratitude and hope. While our paths may never truly align, their impact lingers—The Mona is my tribute to that experience, a way to hold onto the positivity they brought into my life and to share it with others in a way that continues to inspire.

The Project

Why this matters and what’s coming next.

Why This Project Matters

The Mona isn't just a game; it's a tool for connection. In a digital world, The Mona brings people together physically. It’s fully customizable and designed for every occasion.

Future Goals

As the project evolves, the goal is to continue improving and adding new features to enhance the experience.

I’m excited to see how The Mona will be used in the future and hope it brings joy to many gatherings.
