The Story

Every great project starts with an idea. Here's the journey of The Mona.

Inspiration

This project was born out of a love for creativity, technology, and the joy of bringing people together. The initial spark for The Mona came when we encountered 'Odil de Krokodil, a concept that quickly became a beloved part of our friend group. Inspired by the positive reception, we created our own version, "Lucien," which was met with even more enthusiasm.

Why "The Mona"?

When searching for a name, I wanted something that reflected a meaningful source of joy and inspiration in my life. The Mona carries the memory of someone who, in ways she may never fully realize, reignited a light in me I thought was lost. She reminded me of the quiet beauty in small moments and the strength in simply feeling alive again.

This project captures the essence of those feelings, transforming a fleeting connection into a creative expression of gratitude and hope. While our paths may never truly align, the impact lingers—The Mona is my tribute to that experience, a way to hold onto the positivity it brought into my life and to share it with others in a way that continues to inspire and create lasting moments together.

The Project

A game, a tool, an experience – built for every occasion.

Project Vision

The Mona isn't just a game; it's an interactive experience designed to bring people together. Whether it's a high-energy drinking game, a casual quiz night, or a competitive challenge, The Mona adapts to any gathering.
With its wireless controls and dynamic game modes, it’s more than just entertainment—it’s a way to connect.

Features of The Mona
Play your way

Drinking games, quizzes, or friendly competitions—The Mona does it all.

Instant fun

Connect to the Pi’s local WiFi, pick a game in the web app, and start playing—zero setup required.

Wireless freedom

Six totally untethered buttons mean no messy cables—just smooth, chaotic fun.

Built to grow

New game modes? Custom rules? The Mona evolves with you!

DIY & Budget-friendly

Affordable, open-source, and ready for anyone who loves to tweak and tinker.

Party-Ready

Whether it’s a casual night in or an epic celebration, The Mona fits right in.

Future goals

The journey doesn’t stop here! The Mona will continue to grow with more game modes, enhanced features, and even broader applications beyond party games. The vision is to keep refining and expanding The Mona’s potential, ensuring it remains the go-to interactive gaming experience for any event.

Special thanks

A heartfelt thank you to everyone who tested the early versions of The Mona and provided invaluable feedback. Your insights and enthusiasm have helped shape this project into what it is today.

Thank you for being a part of this journey!
