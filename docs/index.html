Story

Inspiration


This project was born out of a love for creativity, technology, and the joy of bringing people together.
The initial spark for The Mona came after we encountered "Odil de Krokodil," a concept that quickly became a beloved part of our friend group.
Inspired by the positive reception, we created our own version, "Lucien," which was met with even more enthusiasm.

"The Mona"


When searching for a name, I wanted something that reflected a meaningful source of joy and inspiration in my life.
The Mona carries the memory of someone who, in ways they may never fully realize, reignited a light in me I thought was lost.
They reminded me of the quiet beauty in small moments and the strength in simply feeling alive again.

This project captures the essence of those feelings, transforming a fleeting connection into a creative expression of gratitude and hope.
While our paths may never truly align, their impact lingers—The Mona is my tribute to that experience,
a way to hold onto the positivity they brought into my life and to share it with others in a way that continues to inspire.

Project

Why This Project Matters


The Mona isn't just a game; it's a tool for connection.
In a world where digital interactions often replace face-to-face connections, The Mona encourages people to engage with each other in a lively, playful manner.
It’s designed to be open-source and customizable, making it accessible and adaptable for everyone.

Special Thanks


A special thanks to everyone who tested the early versions of The Mona and provided feedback.
Your insights have been instrumental in shaping this project into what it is today.

Future Goals

As this project evolves, the goal is to continue improving and adding new features that enhance the user experience.
I’m excited to see how The Mona will be used in the future and hope it brings joy to many gatherings.
